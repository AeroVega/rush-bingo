Field Guide

Card rule: the concert comes first. This is a side quest for waiting, slow parts, intermission, and the post-concert comedown. If something amazing is happening onstage, the Bingo square can wait.

Mark only things you genuinely witness. Don’t manufacture a square, interrupt anyone, or spend the concert staring at the phone.

RUSH SCHOLAR

Someone demonstrates unsolicited, highly specific Rush knowledge: album history, trivia, musical details, deep cuts, or lore.

OLD-SCHOOL FAN

Evidence of a long personal history with Rush: vintage concert stories, old shows, vintage merch, or similar first-hand history. Simply looking old does not count.

GEAR NERD

Someone demonstrates unusually specific interest in guitars, basses, drums, pedals, amps, stage equipment, or identifying particular gear.

FREE SPACE

Someone air-drums. It’s Rush. This one is basically a law of physics.

What does not count

  • Something either of you intentionally does.
  • Something you only hear about afterward.
  • Something you think might have happened but did not actually witness.
  • Generic concert behavior unless the square itself makes it Rush-specific.
